# App icon generation

Tool: built-in imagegen. Reference: previous green hand-and-window app icon.

## Production prompt

Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand.
Edit the reference into the final production macOS application icon for Wally's Hand, matching its new restrained AXO design language.
Keep the recognizable motif: one open hand cradling a small desktop window to its upper right. Simplify it into a highly polished, minimal, nearly flat graphic with precise smooth contours and generous negative space, legible at 32px.
Replace forest green, mint green and cream entirely: full-bleed solid pearl pale blue background #F1F6FC; blue-black #10151F hand silhouette; white #FFFFFF window body with a thin quiet blue-gray border #DDE7F2; muted deep blue #3363AD window title bar with three small pale dots. Remove decorative rays. Gentle restrained edge depth only, no puffy toy rendering, no heavy shadows, no gloss, no glow, no gradients, no texture.
Centered balanced symbol occupying approximately 72% of the canvas. Square 1024x1024 asset. Background extends fully to all four image edges: do NOT draw an outer rounded icon tile, do NOT include rounded transparent corners, external whitespace, a device mockup or perspective view. macOS will supply the outer squircle mask. No letters, text, watermark or other elements. Preserve the hand plus window brand identity, but give it a rational, clean, contemporary productivity-tool finish.
